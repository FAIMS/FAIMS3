# Sketch

_How to add and configure a Sketch field in the {{Notebook}} Editor._

---

## What This Field Does

A Sketch field opens a full-screen drawing lightbox. Collectors can draw with
ink, lines, rectangles, and ellipses, choose colours and stroke width, and
undo or clear. Strokes are saved into the record as editable drawing data
while the collector works. A PNG preview is written when they save or close
the lightbox, and that image syncs like a photo.

## Adding the Field

To add this field, open the
[ADD A FIELD dialog](../shared-settings/adding-a-field.md), navigate to the
**MEDIA** tab, and click the **Sketch** card.

## Configuring the Field

Click the field's **grey header bar** to expand it. Sketch uses the shared
field settings only — Label, Helper Text, Field ID, Required, Annotation,
Uncertainty, and Conditions. See
[Field Identity](../shared-settings/field-identity.md) and
[Field Options](../shared-settings/field-options.md).

## Tips

- **Use Sketch for field drawings** (profiles, artefact outlines, site
  sketches). Use [Take Photo](take-photo.md) for camera capture.
- A new sketch uses the current screen shape (tall on a portrait phone,
  wide on a tablet or desktop). Re-opening a saved drawing keeps that
  original size.
- Closing the lightbox or tapping **Save** writes the PNG. Stroke data is
  saved continuously so a crash does not lose the drawing.
- On another device the PNG may show as unavailable until attachment
  download is enabled in Settings. The drawing can still be edited from
  the saved strokes.
